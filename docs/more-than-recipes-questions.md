# More Than Recipes — the six lightbulb questions

Saved Nov 2026 when the /myrecipe homepage entrance changed to the two image cards
(Explore Your Kitchen / Discover More Than Recipes). These were the "benefit sparks"
shown under the headline. Kept word for word for the future More Than Recipes
presentation (slides, demonstrations or conversation starters).

1. 💡 Tired of hunting through Pinterest, Facebook, and websites for that one recipe?
2. 💡 Ever wonder what you could make with what’s already in your kitchen or create your own signature recipe?
3. 💡 Wish you had someone to bounce cooking ideas off?
4. 💡 Want to adjust a dish to match your family’s tastes or health needs?
5. 💡 Wish you could stretch one chicken into several meals?
6. 💡 Still have Mom’s handwritten recipes? Wish you could preserve the recipes and memories behind them?
